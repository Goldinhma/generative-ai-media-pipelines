# Generative AI Media & Video Production Pipelines

Architectural documentation, production frameworks, and model benchmarking for commercial-grade AI video generation, synthetic audio, and dynamic visual systems.

---

## 1. Production Pipeline Architecture

### Phase A: Concept & Narrative Logic
* **Script & Scene Breakdown:** Narrative pacing, shot list generation, and continuity mapping.
* **Prompt Frameworks:** Structured parameter tuning (aspect ratios, motion vectors, lens simulation, camera pan/tilt controls).

### Phase B: Multimodal Asset Synthesis
* **Visual Generation:** Benchmarking and generating high-coherence keyframes and video sequences using frontier models (Midjourney, Kling AI, Sora, Nano Banana, Google Veo).
* **Audio Synthesis:** Synthetic voice design, emotive cadence matching, and speech alignment via ElevenLabs.

### Phase C: Post-Processing & Packaging
* **Compositing & Interpolation:** Frame upscaling, artifact correction, and pacing refinement in DaVinci Resolve & After Effects.
* **Graphic Packaging:** Title styling, brand identity alignment, and platform-native aspect ratio adaptations (16:9 commercial broadcast vs. 9:16 short-form).

---

## 2. Frontier Toolchain & Environment

* **Generative Engines:** Midjourney, Kling AI, Google Veo, Sora, Nano Banana, ElevenLabs
* **Post-Production & VFX:** DaVinci Resolve, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro
* **Design & Systems:** Adobe Creative Suite, CorelDRAW
* **Hardware Specs:** Intel Core i7 (12th Gen), 32 GB DDR5 RAM, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 Ti (8 GB VRAM)

---

## 3. Benchmarking Focus Areas

1. **Temporal Coherence:** Minimizing character morphing across multi-shot sequences.
2. **Camera Mechanics:** Replicating physical cinematic camera moves (dolly zoom, parallax, steadycam) strictly through text and image-to-video guidance.
3. **Turnaround Efficiency:** Engineering prompt libraries that reduce concept-to-final-render turnaround by over 50%.
